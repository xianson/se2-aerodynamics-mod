Where the game's thrust acts, how thrusters and gyros steer, thrust-vs-Mach curves, flames.

## Entry points
- `ThrustTorque.Apply` (`AeroGridComponent.AttitudeJob`, after the game's grid systems, before physics).
- `OffsetThrustJob.RebuildThrusterCache` / `RebuildGyroCache` (the sim job, when dirty).
- `AeroPropulsorComponent` (both scenes, before the simulation): the maxima, the governed rpm (server); then `ReversibleThrustComponent` (its game side, `ThrustData.Direction`).
- `AeroRotorStrikeComponent.OnStrikeProbe` (server, every tick, after `GridUpdateOrder.UpdateSystems`, before the physics step): the rotor-strike rays; then `AeroGridComponent.OnRotorStrike` (the first server grid's, once a tick, after the probes and the attitude jobs): the strikes' consequences.
- `AeroPropulsorComponent.OnRamjetFuel` (server, every tick, before the game's `GridUpdateOrder.ResourceDistributorUpdate`): a ramjet's hydrogen burn held to its thrust share.
- `AeroRamjetSpikeComponent` (client render, after `RenderSubmissionBegin`): the ramjets' inlet spikes.
- `AeroSolidNozzleComponent` / `AeroSolidSegmentComponent` (before the simulation): the solid motors' stacks, ignition, thrust, tank reads, put-backs and mass; `AeroSolidNozzleComponent.OnSolidFuel` (before `GridUpdateOrder.ResourceDistributorUpdate`): the burn's duty cycle.
- Injected: `AeroThrustSettingsComponent` (thrusters), `AeroFlameComponent` (client thrusters), `AeroFuelMassComponent` (tanks - not the solid rocket segments', which weigh through their own component), the solid rocket components (`SolidRocketBlocks` prefabs).

## Threading
- `AeroSimJob` (a DCS job per grid, both scenes, in parallel across grids): the cache rebuilds; the attitude job (after the grid systems, before physics) calls `ThrustTorque.Apply`, which uses
  `ThrustAllocator` and `GyroControl`. Statics touched from there are per-grid records under their own locks.
- Client render jobs (after `RenderSubmissionBegin`): `AeroFlameComponent` reads the shares `ThrustTorque` published.
- A reversible thruster's force is signed along its `ThrustDirection` everywhere it is read: `LastThrustShares` (StructureServer loads), the API's `GetThrusterForces` (negative: the other way), `DesignSnapshot.ThrRev` (both ways in `ThrustAlong` and `ThrustHold`). `ThrustTorque` reads its game side from its live `ThrustData.Direction` (`LiveMax`).
- Reflection used here is listed in `docs/REFLECTION_AND_WHITELIST.md`.
